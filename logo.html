Concept 01 · Symbol + Wordmark
The AR Targeting Frame
Top Pick Layered · Technical
Dark · Primary
Light · Secondary
App Icon · Avatar
Concept & Background

The AR Targeting Frame is inspired by the viewfinder/targeting reticle of an Augmented Reality HUD display. The three nested squares (outer frame, middle layer, inner glow) represent the layered depth of AR — the physical world, the digital overlay, and the focal point of attention. The crosshair lines echo scanning and precision alignment.

The 'Pakkar' Hidden Hook

The square frame with its grid structure visually echoes the Urdu letterform of "پ" (Pe) — the first letter of Pakkar — when the crosshairs and center dot are read as a script reference. To insiders, the mark "grips" the subject at the center, reinforcing the Pakkar (to seize) meaning without being literal.

Why It Works for PAK-AR

The nested square icon reduces beautifully to a 16×16 favicon and app icon while retaining all its meaning. The symbol + wordmark format allows the hyphen in PAK-AR to visually split into the icon and name — PAK [icon] AR. The teal-on-black rendering is exceptional at every scale.

Brand Impact

Immediately signals augmented reality without being generic. The layered frame structure communicates precision engineering and technological authority — exactly the right signal for B2B enterprise clients who need to trust a technology partner from the first glance.

Concept 02 · Symbol + Wordmark
The Hexagonal AR Core
Strong Contender Geometric · Layered
Dark · Primary
Light · Secondary

Concept & Background

The hexagon is the most structurally efficient shape in nature — used in everything from molecular chemistry to drone sensor arrays to QR code matrices. Three nested hexagons create a dimensional depth that perfectly represents AR layering: physical world, digital overlay, and the augmented focal core. The "AR" lettermark sits at the center as a powerful dual-purpose element.

The 'Pakkar' Hidden Hook

The three concentric hexagonal layers visually mirror the Urdu writing motion of "پَکَّڑ" (Pakkar) — a gripping, enclosing gesture. The innermost hexagon "holds" the AR mark at its center, embodying the concept of Pakkar (to grasp, to hold). The brand is literally gripping the AR technology at its core.

Why It Works for PAK-AR

The hexagon has a rich visual language in tech: blockchain, molecular science, sensor grids, honeycomb structures. It reads as systematic and engineered — ideal for a B2B WebAR platform. The "AR" embedded in the icon means the symbol works standalone as a powerful app icon, instantly communicating the brand's category.

Brand Impact

Memorably geometric and distinctive from any other AR company in Pakistan or globally. The layered hexagon occupies visual space differently from competitor logos, creating instant category ownership. Scales well from favicon to billboard.

The P·AR Overlay
Most Unique Lettermark · Clever
Dark · Primary

App Icon
Concept & Background

The P·AR Overlay takes the letter "P" — standing for both Pakistan and the phonetic start of "Pakkar" — and layers an AR scanning frame directly inside the bowl of the P. The dashed rectangular overlay inside the P represents a live WebAR scan in progress. The horizontal scan line below the bowl completes the scanning metaphor.

The 'Pakkar' Hidden Hook

This is the strongest Pakkar reference in the set. The "P" is literally the first letter of "Pakkar" (پَکَّڑ). The AR scan frame embedded within the P bowl represents the act of "Pakkar" — the brand seizing and capturing products in the AR frame. Every scan is a "Pakkar" — a grip on reality.

Why It Works for PAK-AR

The cleverness of embedding an AR frame inside the P creates a logo that rewards closer inspection — a powerful trait for social media where people slow down to look twice. The scan line below the bowl creates a natural baseline that makes the logo feel grounded and systematic.

Brand Impact

Highly ownable and unique in the AR industry globally. No competitor uses a lettermark-with-AR-overlay concept. The logo literally shows what the product does — it overlays AR onto reality — making it the most self-explanatory logo in this report.

The QR Portal Mark
Most On-Brand Abstract · Conceptual
Dark · Primary
Light · Secondary
App Icon
Concept & Background

The QR Portal Mark abstracts the QR code — PAK-AR's core product mechanism — into a brand symbol. It preserves the four corner "finder patterns" that make any QR code instantly recognizable, while replacing the internal data squares with a branded geometric composition. The result is a mark that IS the product concept.

The 'Pakkar' Hidden Hook

The four corner marks "grip" the center composition from all sides — a visual Pakkar in four directions. In Urdu typography, "پَکَّڑ" implies enclosure and control from multiple points. This mark literally holds and frames reality from four corners, mirroring how a QR code captures a portal into the AR world.

Why It Works for PAK-AR

The QR pattern is a cultural icon in Pakistan's digital-first SME market. Business owners recognize QR corners instantly — making this logo immediately legible as "the AR QR company." The abstract internal grid creates depth and layering while keeping the mark clean and modern.

Brand Impact

Most directly communicates PAK-AR's product — QR-to-AR experiences. The corner patterns create an unmistakable silhouette that reads at any size. The tagline "Scan to Experience" paired with this mark creates a complete brand narrative in one visual unit.

05
Concept 05 · Enhanced Wordmark
The Layered Wordmark
Most Versatile Wordmark · Dimensional
Dark · Primary
Light · Secondary
App Icon
Concept & Background

The Layered Wordmark takes Chakra Petch's naturally technical letterforms and adds dimensional depth through a subtle offset shadow layer (Deep Teal offset behind Pure White "PAK"). The hyphen is transformed from a punctuation mark into a three-line scan beam — the most product-literal element in the entire word. "AR" is set in Core Teal, splitting the brand name into its two meanings.

The 'Pakkar' Hidden Hook

The offset shadow behind "PAK" creates an optical illusion of the word "grabbing" its own shadow — a Pakkar (a grip or hold) on its own identity. When read as "PAK grips AR," the layered typography physically enacts the brand name. The scan beam hyphen echoes the quick strike motion of "Pakkar" — a moment of capture.

Why It Works for PAK-AR

The wordmark approach is the most versatile of all five concepts — it requires no icon file and works across every medium from embroidery on T-shirts to screen-printed packaging to digital banners. The dimensional shadow effect is subtle enough to remain professional while adding just enough visual intelligence.

Brand Impact

The scan beam hyphen is the signature detail — it makes the logo feel alive and in-motion. When a client sees this on a pitch deck, the horizontal scan lines immediately suggest a live AR scan in progress, reinforcing the brand promise before a single word is read.

✦ Strategic Logo Recommendation
The AR Targeting Frame — Concept 01

Based on PAK-AR's priorities — app icon and social media avatar performance, bold & futuristic first impression, Both dark and light contexts, and the Pakkar hidden hook — Concept 01 (The AR Targeting Frame) is the strongest strategic choice.

The nested square targeting frame reduces to a perfect app icon at 16×16px while retaining full meaning at hero scale. It works flawlessly on both Absolute Black and Pure White in the confirmed High Contrast palette. The crosshair and center dot create an authentic AR HUD aesthetic that no competitor in Pakistan owns.

Recommended combination: Use Concept 01 as your primary icon mark, and pair it with Concept 05's scan-beam wordmark treatment for full horizontal logo lockups. This gives PAK-AR a complete two-mode logo system — a standalone icon for app/avatar contexts and a full wordmark for enterprise materials.
